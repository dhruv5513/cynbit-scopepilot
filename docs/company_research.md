# Cynbit Technologies – Company Research

## 1. Company Overview

Cynbit Technologies operates in the technology and digital solutions space, offering services related to software development, AI automation, cloud solutions, SaaS products, and digital marketing.

The company focuses on helping businesses improve their digital operations through technology-driven solutions.

Its service portfolio includes custom software development, AI-powered automation, autonomous agents, and business workflow optimization.

## 2. Business Areas

The company's publicly presented business areas include:

- Artificial Intelligence and Automation
- Custom Software Development
- SaaS Product Development
- Cloud Solutions
- Digital Marketing and CRM Automation

## 3. Research Scope

This research focuses on understanding how Cynbit handles incoming client requirements and how AI-assisted tools could improve its initial requirement analysis and project planning process.

The analysis is based on publicly available company information. Internal workflows and operational challenges have not been independently verified.

## 4. Existing Products Analysis

Cynbit's publicly presented product portfolio includes the following products:

### 4.1 Cynbit Studio

Cynbit Studio focuses on coding practice, AI-based code evaluation, career preparation, and job matching.

### 4.2 TenantEase

TenantEase is focused on property and tenant management, including payment tracking and occupancy-related operations.

### 4.3 Revio

Revio focuses on online reputation and review management, including customer feedback analysis and review response workflows.

### 4.4 Product Analysis and Opportunity

The existing products address specific business and user requirements.

The proposed ScopePilot system will have a different purpose. It will focus on analysing incoming client project inquiries, identifying incomplete requirements, suggesting clarification questions, and preparing an initial technical project brief.

This proposed solution is intended for internal pre-sales and project discovery assistance rather than replacing any existing Cynbit product.
